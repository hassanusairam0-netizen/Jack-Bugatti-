    03 / Power Delivery

    “Power means nothing without precision.”

    From the engine, torque passes through a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission. A share is sent rearward through the differential and half-shafts; the rest travels forward along a propeller shaft to the front axle — so all four wheels pull together.
